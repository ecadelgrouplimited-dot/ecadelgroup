Who We Are

A new kind of
technology institution

ECADEL GROUP LIMITED is a digital infrastructure and systems conglomerate headquartered in Kampala, Uganda. We are builders of foundational technology — platforms, tools, and intelligence systems that do not follow existing categories, but create new ones.

Our portfolio spans five distinct subsidiaries: a business operating system for African SMEs, an agentic AI extended-brain platform, a road safety infrastructure layer, a city-intelligence and local discovery network, and a consequence intelligence platform designed to transform how governments and corporations make decisions.

We build for Africa first — because Africa's problems are the world's hardest problems, and the companies that solve them will define the next century of global technology.

Beyond our subsidiary portfolio, ECADEL GROUP delivers client-facing technology services — mobile and web development, custom software, hosting, technology consultancy, and AI training and integration — for organisations across Africa and beyond.

A name crafted from the founders themselves — hidden in plain sight

ECADEL was not named by a branding agency or generated by an algorithm. The name was crafted — assembled from the syllables and initials of the two people who built it: Wilson Ecaat and Catherine Ademun. A permanent, invisible inscription of identity in the institution they are building together.

The company was registered in Uganda in 2026, after a journey through the Uganda Registration Services Bureau that tested patience and resolve — and produced a name that tells a story no competitor can claim. A name that sounds like a global technology institution. A name that carries a legacy, even before the legacy is fully written.

What began as a solo founder's drive to build systems that solve real problems in the real Africa became something larger: a conglomerate thesis. Five companies. Five layers of infrastructure. One coherent mission.

Build the systems Africa runs on. Then let Africa show the world what's possible.
